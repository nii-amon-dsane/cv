# Application conventions
- `profile/cv.md` and `profile/achievements.md` are the truth source. Do not add claims, titles, dates, companies, skills, or metrics without evidence.
- Keep application-specific artifacts in their own folder. Preserve source job material as `jd.txt`.
- Use `meta.json` for source, ATS setting, fit, gaps, confirmation, and application state.
- Apply the voice rules in `profile/style_guide.md`; use short, factual, outcome-first copy.
- Company research must cite URLs and mark unverified facts.
