# Interview Prep: OpenFn - Managing Director, Services

## Positioning in one sentence

I have spent two decades turning complex operating problems into delivered technology across government, digital health, fintech and shared-services environments, while leading managers, owning delivery economics and remaining technically credible on integration architecture.

## The five stories to prepare

### 1. Ghana biometric voter registration - national-scale government delivery
Use for: government stakeholders, national-scale systems, implementation risk, technical credibility.

Key facts:
- Lead Software Developer at GenKey Africa.
- Worked on ABIS software and the Biometric Voter Register Adjudication Client.
- Direct technical engagement with Ghana's Electoral Commission.
- Trained key trainers for biometric registration.
- Programme scale: approximately 14M voters and 6,500 registration kits.

What to emphasize:
- Public-sector delivery is not just software delivery; it includes training, operational readiness, stakeholder alignment and failure consequences.

### 2. Living Goods - digital health and distributed delivery
Use for: NGO/global health context, team leadership, technical assistance, distributed work.

Key facts:
- Led software engineering for direct operations and technical-assistance programmes.
- Team covered engineering, data science, QA, design, product and project management.
- Hired and managed Engineering Managers.
- Established agile/continuous-delivery practices.

What to emphasize:
- The feedback loop between field operations, programme data, product decisions and technical delivery.

### 3. Radio Africa - shared-services operating model
Use for: Services organisation, standardization, portfolio oversight, leadership bench.

Key facts:
- Led 12 developers, designers and web administrators.
- Team operated as shared services across the group.
- Managed engineering leadership.
- Advised Group CEO/C-suite on digital transition.

What to emphasize:
- Shared services need clear intake, prioritization, standards, service ownership and escalation paths or they become an internal queue of bespoke work.

### 4. Peach / Bridge - financial accountability and leadership of managers
Use for: gross margin/cost discipline, budgets, executive accountability, delivery economics.

Verified fact:
- Direct responsibility for budgets, delivery costs and financial targets at both Peach and Bridge.

Prepare concrete examples before interview:
- What budget/cost categories did you own?
- How did you review actual vs plan?
- What decisions did you make when costs or delivery deviated?
- How did financial targets affect hiring, sequencing, vendor spend or scope?
- What did you report to CEO/founders/executive team?

Do not invent percentages or margin values if you do not remember them.

### 5. MTN/MFS Africa + KCB - complex scoping and partner delivery
Use for: pre-sale scoping, technical sign-off, multi-stakeholder risk.

Key facts:
- Led technical delivery for cross-border remittance integration with MTN Group/MFS Africa.
- Delivered KCB integration for outbound remittances to Nigeria.
- Owned technical feasibility, architecture, scope, timelines and resourcing.
- Commercial price and contract negotiation was handled by other leaders.

Good framing:
"I have repeatedly been the technical/delivery authority in complex partner engagements. I would not claim that I was the commercial negotiator; my responsibility was making sure what we committed to was technically and operationally real."

## Likely questions

### Why OpenFn, and why this role rather than another CTO role?
Answer around the combination of:
- public-service infrastructure;
- integration/interoperability;
- leadership of a delivery organisation;
- business-model transition;
- AI adoption grounded in real workflows.

Avoid framing it as a fallback from CTO roles.

### How would you transition Services from projects to subscription-shaped delivery?
Suggested structure:
1. Establish baseline economics: revenue mix, gross margin, utilization, SLA performance, rework, write-offs and delivery variance.
2. Segment services into repeatable vs genuinely bespoke work.
3. Define standard service units and entitlement boundaries for Expert Days/support.
4. Tighten pre-sale scoping and delivery sign-off before contracts are committed.
5. Create playbooks, reusable implementation assets and AI-assisted knowledge workflows.
6. Clarify RACI between Growth, Services, Product and partners.
7. Run the new model as a measured pilot before broad rollout.

### What would you measure?
- Gross margin by service type / subscription bundle.
- Delivery cost vs plan.
- Expert Days consumption and rollover/overage patterns.
- Utilization and capacity by role.
- Scope variance / change-request frequency.
- Time to implementation and SLA attainment.
- Rework and escalation rate.
- Partner-delivered vs OpenFn-delivered work.
- Product feedback converted into reusable platform capability.
- Customer outcomes where measurable.

### How would you work with Growth?
Position Services as the technical/delivery authority, not the deal owner:
- standard scoping framework;
- capacity and risk checks;
- delivery sign-off on scope/timeline/resourcing;
- explicit assumptions and exclusions;
- feedback on pricing economics;
- no silent acceptance of over-commitment.

### How would you handle the commercial-negotiation gap?
Be direct:
"Commercial negotiation has normally been owned by founders or commercial leaders. My role has been the senior technical and delivery voice: feasibility, scope, architecture, timeline and resourcing. I am comfortable with the financial side and have owned budgets, delivery costs and targets. The contract-negotiation component is an area I would step into more directly here."

### How do you think governments should adopt AI?
Use your existing principle:
- LLMs for interpretation, generation and unstructured inputs.
- Deterministic systems for transactions, state, policy rules and audit-critical decisions.
- Human review where consequences are high.
- Data sovereignty, observability and clear failure modes.
- Start from public-service workflow outcomes, not from an AI feature.

## First 90 days

### Days 1-30 - understand
- Review portfolio economics and delivery health.
- Meet Services leaders, Product, Growth, COO and CEO.
- Sample active government/NGO implementations and escalations.
- Understand current scoping, SLA, Expert Days and partner workflows.
- Identify where delivery knowledge is trapped in individuals.

### Days 31-60 - design
- Define service taxonomy and delivery/financial metrics.
- Tighten pre-sale delivery sign-off.
- Draft Growth/Services/Product operating boundaries.
- Identify repeatable implementation patterns for playbooks and productization.
- Select 1-2 AI-assisted internal workflows with measurable value.

### Days 61-90 - operationalize
- Run portfolio review cadence against agreed metrics.
- Pilot revised subscription-delivery model on a small set of accounts.
- Establish leadership goals and accountability.
- Publish first version of implementation/service playbook changes.
- Present structural gaps and next-quarter priorities to Global Strategy Team.

## Questions to ask OpenFn

1. What are the current gross-margin and delivery-efficiency targets for Services, and where is performance furthest from target today?
2. What percentage of Services revenue is still project-based versus bundled/subscription-shaped?
3. What is the current failure mode in scoping: underestimation, unclear assumptions, capacity, client change, or something else?
4. What decision rights will the Managing Director have over scope sign-off, staffing and pricing inputs?
5. How are Expert Days currently packaged, consumed and measured?
6. Where does the Head of Implementation's remit end and this role's begin in practice?
7. Which implementation knowledge do you most want to turn into product or repeatable playbooks?
8. How much delivery do you expect regional implementation partners to own in 12-24 months?
9. What would make you say after six months that this hire changed the economics and quality of Services?
10. What prompted the role now: scale, margin pressure, organisational separation from Growth, or all three?

## Risk reminders

- Do not claim commercial price/contract negotiation ownership.
- Do not claim a prior identical project-to-subscription Services transition.
- Do not claim French or Spanish unless separately verified.
- Keep AI discussion grounded in workflows and operational outcomes.
