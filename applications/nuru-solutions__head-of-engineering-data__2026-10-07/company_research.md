# Company research — Nuru Solutions

## Snapshot

Nuru Solutions is a Nairobi-based agricultural data-intelligence company serving lenders, insurers and agribusinesses. Its products combine satellite imagery, weather data, ground-truth data and predictive models to provide farm-level risk profiles, yield forecasts, boundary detection, crop-health monitoring and climate insights.

The current website reports 25K+ farmers impacted, 8K+ acres digitized and operations/impact across three countries. It lists partners including FSD Africa, Delta40, Mastercard, Seedstars Ventures and World Vision.

## Product and market

Nuru's current product is a web dashboard and data platform. The Head of Engineering & Data role is intended to turn that into commercial-grade, multi-channel products spanning web, field-team mobile tools and farmer-facing messaging while automating data/ML pipelines and reducing manual refinement.

A current Nuru ML hiring brief describes the wider platform as serving Kenya, Malawi, Nigeria and Somalia and says the company is formalising ML governance, reproducibility and production pipelines. This indicates the engineering leader will inherit a product with validated technical capability but immature production/process infrastructure.

## Role context

The mandate covers technology strategy, product development, engineering, data architecture, pipelines, ML integration, cloud infrastructure, team building, IP custody and CEO/operations partnership. The first-year emphasis is automation, unit economics, commercial-grade infrastructure and scaling without proportional headcount growth.

## Company-stage signal

Delta40 lists Nuru as a portfolio company. Delta40 describes itself as a venture studio and VC focused on building and scaling African ventures. The role therefore appears to be an early-stage build-and-professionalise mandate rather than a mature engineering-management position.
