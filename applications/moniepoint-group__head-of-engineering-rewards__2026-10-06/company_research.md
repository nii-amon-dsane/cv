# Company Research: Moniepoint Group

Date: 2026-10-06. Depth: standard.

## Snapshot

Moniepoint provides payments, banking, credit and business-management products across Africa. It is expanding to Kenya after its Sumac Microfinance Bank acquisition.

## Product and engineering signals

The Rewards platform currently runs cashback and is intended to grow into retention, engagement and personalisation products. Its user base is stated as about 23 million people.

## Why this role is open

The post describes a new dedicated charter with a 9-person team and a target of at least 3 product units and 25+ people.

## Application hooks

- Lead with consumer products, marketplace delivery and fintech systems.
- Lead with Bridge's consumer apps, merchant app and API suite.
- State Engineering Manager hiring and management directly.
- Do not claim A/B testing, feature flags or formal experimentation that are not in the CV source.
