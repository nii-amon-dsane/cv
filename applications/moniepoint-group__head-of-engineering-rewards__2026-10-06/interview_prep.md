# Interview prep: Rewards

## Core stories
- Bridge consumer apps, merchant app and payment APIs.
- Peach marketplace product and operating-system delivery.
- Radio Africa consumer streaming, content-ingestion and mobile-money billing products.
- Rancard multi-country consumer messaging products.

## Questions
- How would you improve cashback? Start with reliability, customer journey and clear measures.
- How would you establish experimentation? Be direct that formal A/B testing is not in your source history; explain the operating model you would build.
- Where would you use language models? Use them for interpretation, generation and customer insight. Keep reward rules, balances and financial controls in conventional systems.
