# Job Description: Moniepoint Group — Head of Engineering, Rewards

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4463978915/
Captured: 2026-10-06

## Role

Lead a cashback platform that Moniepoint plans to grow into rewards, retention, engagement and personalisation products for businesses, consumers and sales agents. The platform serves about 23 million people. The role starts with 9 engineers, including an Engineering Manager, and should grow to at least 3 product units and 25+ people.

## Responsibilities

- Own technical strategy, architecture and roadmap.
- Improve cashback performance, reliability and product depth.
- Build rewards and retention products with Growth, Sales and Marketing.
- Build the engineering organisation and lead through Engineering Managers.
- Establish experimentation, A/B testing, cohort analysis and measurement.
- Own mobile and web customer experience.
- Define architecture, process, quality and engineering-health metrics.

## Requirements

- Direct-to-consumer product engineering at scale.
- Front-end and mobile depth.
- Hands-on A/B testing, feature flags and measurement frameworks.
- 5+ years managing managers.
- 10+ years of software engineering and 5+ years leading teams.
- Java and Spring Boot credibility.
- Fintech, payments, rewards or growth-platform experience is advantageous.
