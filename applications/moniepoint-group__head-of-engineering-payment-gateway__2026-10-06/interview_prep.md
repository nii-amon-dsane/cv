# Interview prep: Payment Gateway

## Core stories

- MTN/MFS Africa: cross-border remittances and multi-party integration.
- KCB: outbound remittances to Nigeria.
- Kwara: core-banking platform architecture and operational delivery for cooperative financial institutions.
- Bridge: organisation design, manager hiring and accelerated product delivery.
- CoreNett: payments, cardless ATM, mobile wallet and Java.

## Likely questions

- How would you improve payment reliability and routing? Use CoreNett and Bridge architecture experience.
- How would you organise Payment In, Payout and developer-platform teams? Explain clear ownership, service objectives and manager accountability.
- How would you make APIs easier to integrate? Discuss versioning, documentation, SDKs, observability and partner feedback.
- What is your Java depth? Prepare specific systems from Rancard, Radio Africa and consulting.

## Questions for Moniepoint

- Which reliability and developer-experience measures define success in the first 6 months?
- How are Payment In, Payout and Commerce Tools staffed today?
- Which international markets and currencies are first?
