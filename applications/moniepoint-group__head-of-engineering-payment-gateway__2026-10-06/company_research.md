# Company Research: Moniepoint Group

Date: 2026-10-06. Depth: standard.

## Snapshot

Moniepoint is an African financial platform for payments, banking, credit, cross-border services and business-management tools. It states that it serves 20 million businesses and individuals each month and processes over $250 billion in annual digital-payment value.

## Stage and market

The company operates in Nigeria and is expanding to Kenya after its acquisition of Sumac Microfinance Bank. Its 2025 impact report states that it employs more than 3,300 people in 10 countries. Public valuation and current funding details were not checked.

## Product and engineering signals

The role lists Java, Spring Boot, Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, Elasticsearch, AWS and microservices. Moniepoint publishes engineering material on high availability, Kafka, Java/Spring/Kubernetes and event-driven savings products.

## People and culture

The post identifies the Group CTO and Co-founder as hiring manager. It evaluates customer focus, craft, systemisation, urgency, ownership, no ego, candour and integrity.

## Why this role is open

The stated reason is international expansion and the evolution of Monnify into a multi-currency, multi-market payment platform.
