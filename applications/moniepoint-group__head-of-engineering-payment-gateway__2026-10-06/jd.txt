# Job Description: Moniepoint Group — Head of Engineering, Payment Gateway

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4463992127/
Captured: 2026-10-06

## Role

Lead Monnify, Moniepoint's online payment-acceptance platform. It connects merchants to card networks, banks and payment aggregators. The platform owns transaction reliability, routing, throughput, APIs, SDKs, documentation and integration experience. It is expanding toward multi-currency and multi-market operation.

## Responsibilities

- Own technical strategy, architecture and roadmap across Payment In, Payout, Commerce Tools and developer-facing features.
- Lead multiple engineering teams through Engineering Managers.
- Own developer experience: APIs, documentation, SDKs and integration ease.
- Drive multi-currency and international expansion.
- Ensure reliability, security and operational performance in a regulated environment.
- Partner with product, commercial and operations teams.
- Hire, mentor and develop Engineering Managers and senior engineers.

## Requirements

- 10+ years in software engineering and 5+ years in engineering leadership.
- 5+ years of hands-on production Java.
- 5+ years managing managers.
- Payments, fintech or financial-services experience, including payment flows and compliance.
- API-first product and developer-platform experience.
- Backend depth in high-throughput, distributed transactional systems.
- Payment-gateway or multi-currency-platform experience is an advantage.

## Technology

Java, Spring, Spring Boot, Docker, Kubernetes, PostgreSQL, DynamoDB, Elasticsearch, AWS and microservices.
