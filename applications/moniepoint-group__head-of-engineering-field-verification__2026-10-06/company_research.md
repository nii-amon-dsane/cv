# Company Research: Moniepoint Group

Date: 2026-10-06. Depth: standard.

## Snapshot

Moniepoint provides payments, banking, credit and business-management products across Africa. It is expanding to Kenya after its Sumac Microfinance Bank acquisition.

## Product and engineering signals

Field Verification performs KYC checks for individuals, businesses, addresses and guarantors. It serves onboarding, lending, compliance and fraud through a back office, APIs, webhooks and field-officer tools.

## Why this role is open

The post says the domain has lacked dedicated engineering leadership. The stated mandate is to move it beyond maintenance, add operational optimisation and mobile delivery, and prepare it for multi-country use.

## Application hooks

- Lead with Genkey biometric-registration work and government identity systems.
- Connect MFieldwork to mobile field-data collection.
- Lead with APIs, Java, architecture and cross-functional organisation building.
- State Engineering Manager hiring and management directly.
