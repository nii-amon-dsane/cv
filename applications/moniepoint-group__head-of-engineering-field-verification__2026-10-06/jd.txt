# Job Description: Moniepoint Group — Head of Engineering, Field Verification

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4464537485/
Captured: 2026-10-06

## Role

Lead the platform that performs in-person KYC checks for retail and business customers, owners, addresses and loan guarantors. The domain serves onboarding, loans, compliance and fraud. It includes a back office, field-officer web surface, APIs, webhooks, assignment logic and planned native mobile work.

## Responsibilities

- Own technical strategy, architecture and roadmap.
- Move the platform from maintenance to planned product development.
- Grow the cross-functional team and build Engineering Manager capacity.
- Build route and trip optimisation, task allocation and capacity management.
- Own APIs, webhooks and back-office tools.
- Deliver a native mobile experience for field officers.
- Keep verification data structured and auditable for compliance.
- Build for multi-country expansion.

## Requirements

- 10+ years of software engineering with hands-on production and backend depth in Java and Spring Boot.
- 5+ years of engineering leadership, including Engineering Manager management.
- Strong product and business collaboration.
- Location data, route optimisation, compliance data and KYC/verification experience are advantages, not requirements.
