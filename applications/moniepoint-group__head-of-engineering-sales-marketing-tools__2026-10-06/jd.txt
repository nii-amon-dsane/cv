# Job Description: Moniepoint Group — Head of Engineering, Sales & Marketing Tools

Source: https://www.linkedin.com/jobs/view/4463984823/
Captured: 2026-10-06

## Role

Lead MonieCRM, the in-house workforce-operations platform used by Moniepoint's 50,000-person sales network. The domain includes onboarding, network management, portfolios, performance, goals, compensation, sales support, wallets, commission attribution, cashout, welfare funds, partner management, rewards and marketing technology.

## Responsibilities

- Own the platform modernisation mandate and technical direction.
- Build a high-performing engineering team with managers and senior engineers.
- Deliver dependable tools for commercial teams.
- Maintain financial correctness for wallets, commissions, attribution and cashout.
- Work with commercial, product and operational leaders.

## Evidence expected

- Engineering leadership across multi-team operational platforms.
- Strong business and stakeholder collaboration.
- Financial-system correctness and reliability.
- Organisation building and Engineering Manager leadership.
