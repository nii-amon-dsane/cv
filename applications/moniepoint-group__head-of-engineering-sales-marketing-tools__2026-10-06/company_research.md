# Company Research: Moniepoint Group

Date: 2026-10-06. Depth: standard.

## Snapshot

Moniepoint provides payments, banking, credit and business-management products across Africa. It is expanding to Kenya after its Sumac Microfinance Bank acquisition.

## Product and engineering signals

Sales and Marketing Tools owns MonieCRM, a workforce-operations platform for a 50,000-person sales network. It includes commercial operations, wallets, commissions, cashout, partner management, rewards and marketing technology. Financial correctness is a stated core requirement.

## Why this role is open

The stated mandate is platform modernisation and organisation building for a commercial system at large scale.

## Application hooks

- Lead with Peach's marketplace operating system, internal tools and executive product leadership.
- Connect CoreNett and Bridge payments work to wallets, cashout and financial correctness.
- Lead with commercial stakeholder work at Peach, Kwara and Bridge.
- State Engineering Manager hiring and management directly.
