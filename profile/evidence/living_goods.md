## Public programme-scale evidence during the tenure

### Kenya

During 2020-2021, Living Goods was a technical partner to Kenya's Ministry of Health on national community-health digitization.

- Living Goods supported the Ministry of Health in developing the roadmap for a national electronic Community Health Information System (eCHIS).
- In March 2021, Kenya launched its Community Health Strategy and National Community Health Digitization Strategy. Living Goods was a lead partner in conceptualizing the strategies.
- The national eCHIS plan was intended to digitally enable approximately 95,000-100,000 CHWs across all 47 counties.
- Living Goods' Smart Health app was selected as the reference tool for the national eCHIS prototype and pilot.
- Living Goods also co-implemented digitized community-health programmes with county governments, including Kisumu.

### Uganda

During Nii's tenure, Living Goods operated digitized community-health programmes at substantial scale in Uganda.

- By late 2020, Living Goods' Uganda operation had expanded to more than 4,300 CHWs across 20 districts.
- In July 2020, Living Goods launched a public-private technical-assistance partnership with Uganda's Ministry of Health, Oyam District and Malaria Consortium, using digital mHealth technology for government CHWs and supervisors.
- Living Goods' broader Uganda programme used digital workflows and performance data to support community-health delivery.
- By 2021, Living Goods reported 4,500 digitized CHWs serving 3.6 million people in Uganda. This metric is useful as programme context but extends beyond Nii's April 2021 departure and should not be presented as a personal end-of-tenure metric without qualification.

### Cross-country scale

At the end of 2020, Living Goods reported supporting more than 10,000 CHWs in Kenya and Uganda to deliver care to more than 8 million people.

In 2021, Living Goods reported 11,477 CHWs supported and 8,544,610 people served across its programmes.
