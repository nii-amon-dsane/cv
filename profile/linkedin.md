## About

I build products, technology organisations and the systems that run businesses.

Over two decades, I have worked across marketplaces, fintech, payments, banking, digital identity, media and digital health. My roles have ranged from hands-on software and product development to CTO, VP Engineering and executive leadership.

My work sits at the intersection of product, engineering and business operations:

• **Marketplaces and commerce.** Designed marketplace systems spanning B2B trade finance, managed automotive marketplaces and, more recently, AI-native social and conversational commerce.

• **Fintech and payments.** Built mobile wallets, cardless ATM systems and mobile-payment platforms; designed Islamic trade-finance systems using Murabaha; and led cross-border remittance products and integrations.

• **Large-scale platforms and integrations.** Worked on Ghana's biometric voter-registration programme, telecom and mobile-money infrastructure, digital-health systems and an MTN Group cross-border remittance integration with potential reach to approximately 70M subscribers in Nigeria.

• **Product and engineering leadership.** Led Product, Engineering and IT organisations, hired and managed engineering leaders and worked directly with founders, CEOs and executive teams on product strategy, organisation design and technology direction.

• **Software architecture and hands-on engineering.** Designed and built distributed systems, APIs, marketplace platforms, payment systems and workflow-heavy applications using technologies including Java, Ruby, Kotlin, Go, TypeScript and Python.

More recently, I have focused heavily on AI-native product development. I build systems that combine language models and agentic workflows with conventional software, using each where it performs best. LLMs handle interpretation, generation and ambiguous language; deterministic software handles transactions, workflow state, rules and reliability.

I am most useful where a business problem still needs to be turned into a product, an operating model and a technology system that can actually deliver it.
